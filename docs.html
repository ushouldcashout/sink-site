01

Fables mechanics

Fables is the ve(3,3) DEX on Robinhood Chain. Its token is FABLES. Lock FABLES for two years at auto-max and the lock votes every week on which pools receive emissions.

A voting lock receives, on the pools it votes:

Swap fees, staked liquidity
100%
Swap fees, unstaked liquidity
10%
Incentives
Posted on those gauges
Rebase
Added to locks that voted

The lock is a veFABLES NFT. Auto-max keeps it at full weight until it is switched off. Locks with the same expiry can be merged. There is no vote delegation: only the owner of a lock can vote it. Changing a vote more than once a week is penalised. Emissions to each pool are capped against the fees its staked liquidity earned the week before.

Locked means locked. The FABLES inside a veFABLES position cannot be sold.

02

Sink mechanics

Sink accepts FABLES, or a veFABLES NFT you already hold. Everything deposited is merged into one lock at the maximum, with auto-max on. You receive sFABLES, 1:1 on the FABLES inside.

A deposited NFT goes through four steps, in this order:

  1. Claim any pending rebase on the NFT.
  2. Extend it to the maximum and switch auto-max on.
  3. Merge it into Sink's lock.
  4. Mint sFABLES 1:1 on the FABLES it held.

A six-month lock is extended to auto-max on deposit and arrives with the 80% of the points allocation it claimed.

Sink's lock never unlocks. The contract has no function that turns auto-max off. The only way out of a position is to sell sFABLES.

03

The week

Fables' epoch runs Tuesday to Tuesday. Sink's week is built around it.

Tuesday
Fables epoch opens
Tuesday to Sunday
vlSINK holders vote
Sunday night
Weight that did not vote follows the house allocation: pools ranked by fees plus incentives per vote
Monday
Sink casts the block, once
Tuesday
Fables closes the week
Wednesday
Payouts are claimable; Sink's agent claims and distributes them
04

sFABLES

sFABLES is the receipt. It is liquid and transferable. Each sFABLES stands for one FABLES inside Sink's lock.

The income the block earns comes to sFABLES holders every week, in the tokens it was earned in, net of the platform cut. sFABLES does not vote.

sFABLES can trade below one FABLES, most likely in weeks with thin liquidity. Three things push it back toward par: a pool Sink seeds and defends, SINK paid directly to the liquidity providers of that pool, and the treasury buying under par out of its cut.

05

SINK and vlSINK

SINK is the second token. Liquid SINK earns nothing and votes on nothing.

Lock SINK for a fixed term and it becomes vlSINK. vlSINK votes once per epoch on where the block goes, and receives the platform cut. It does not decay: its weight is the same on the last day of the term as on the first. At the end of the term it unlocks in full.

The team holds a voting key. It submits the tally of vlSINK votes and the house allocation to Fables. It cannot move tokens and cannot change the lock.

06

The platform cut

A fixed share of what the block earns goes to vlSINK lockers. The rest goes to sFABLES holders.

07

Risks

Smart contract
Sink's contracts can have bugs. They are in review, and the addresses below stay empty until it is done.
FABLES price
sFABLES tracks FABLES. If FABLES falls, sFABLES falls with it.
Fables parameters
Fables can change its fee split, emissions, rebase or lock rules. Sink's income changes with them.
Below par
In thin weeks sFABLES can trade below one FABLES. Selling then means selling at a discount.
