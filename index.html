What you deposit, what you hold

You deposit FABLES, or a veFABLES NFT you already hold. Sink merges it into one lock at the maximum, with auto-max on, and gives you sFABLES 1:1 on the FABLES inside. The lock behind sFABLES never unlocks. The contract has no switch to turn auto-max off.

07

One message when deposits open

Leave an X handle or an email. You get one message when the contract is live and deposits are open, and nothing else, ever. If you say how many FABLES you hold or will claim, it is added to the total above and never shown on its own.

The list is a Cloudflare KV store. It is not shared, and it is not a token allocation.

08

Questions

Can I get my FABLES back?

No. The exit is selling sFABLES. The lock has no switch to turn auto-max off.

What keeps sFABLES near one FABLES?

A pool Sink seeds and defends, SINK paid directly to the LPs of that pool, and the treasury buying under par out of its cut.

Is Sink live?

Not yet. The contracts are in build and review. Their addresses and the review report go on the Docs page the day they are published; until then nothing here takes a deposit. Deposits open inside the claim window, 20 Oct to 20 Nov.

Does holding sFABLES give me a vote?

No. sFABLES earns; vlSINK votes.

Can I deposit a six-month lock?

Yes. It is extended to auto-max on deposit and arrives with the 80% it claimed.

I run a pool on Fables. How do I get the block?

Post incentives on your gauge, and keep real staked liquidity in it. The house allocation ranks pools by fees plus incentives per vote, so both move the block toward you. The Docs page has the detail for teams.
