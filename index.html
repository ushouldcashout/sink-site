How it works

Three steps. The third one repeats.

On Fables, locked FABLES votes each week on which pools get the new emissions, and voters collect those pools' trading fees. Sink turns that into a thing you do once.

01

Deposit FABLES

Sink locks it for good and hands you sFABLES, a receipt you can hold, sell or move whenever you like. No four year lock.

02

Sink votes as one block

Every week the whole pool votes together, pointed by vlSINK holders at the pools paying the most. One block moves emissions.

03

You get paid

Trading fees and incentives from the pools the block voted for come back to sFABLES holders and vlSINK lockers. Then it does it again next week.

The week

What happens between two Mondays.

Vote by hand if you want to. If you do not, nothing is lost: an opt-in agent casts your weight for you, claims what it earns, and tells you what it did.

Mon

Epoch opens

Last week's fees and incentives land. The gauge page resets for the new week.

Mon to Wed

Vote by hand

vlSINK holders set where the block goes. Majority of weight decides. Change your mind as often as you like.

Wed

Agent fallback

Opted in and haven't voted? Your weight follows the house allocation: the pools paying the most per vote this week.

Before close

One block cast

Sink casts the whole pool's weight on Fables in a single vote. This is the part a thousand small wallets can't do.

Next Mon

The note

Rewards claimed and credited. Agent users get a short note saying what their votes earned. Repeat.

The product

A gauge page and a Monday note.

That is the whole interface. Pick where the block goes, or let the agent do it, and read one message a week.

Security

Agents can vote. They cannot withdraw.

The agent holds a voting key. That key can do exactly two things, and moving funds is not one of them.

Cast the weekly voteagent, no signature
Claim and distribute rewardsagent, no signature
Change your vote or settingsyou, your signature
Move locked tokens anywherenobody. not us, not the agent

Locked FABLES sits in the lock contract, audited before deposits open. Circuit breakers fail closed: if something looks wrong, Sink skips a vote rather than casting a bad one.

Questions

Things people ask first.

Can I get my FABLES back?

No. The lock is permanent; that is what makes the pool's weight real. What you can do, any time, is sell sFABLES. There is a protocol-owned sFABLES/FABLES pool for exactly that.

Does sFABLES vote?

No. sFABLES earns. vlSINK votes. If you want both, hold both.

Why would I lock SINK instead of FABLES?

One locked FABLES is one vote. One locked SINK steers the whole pool's votes and takes the platform's cut on top. If you care where emissions go, SINK is the cheaper lever by a long way.

Do I have to use the agent?

No. Voting by hand is the default. The agent is for people who would rather not show up every week. Turn it on, turn it off, override it any week.

I run a project with a pool on Fables. How do I get the block's votes?

Hold vlSINK and vote for your pool, or post an incentive on the vote market so the house allocation comes to you. Both are cheaper than trying to out-lock the pool yourself.

What about delegating instead of depositing?

If Fables ships vote delegation, Sink will take delegated weight alongside deposits. Nothing here depends on it being available at launch.

When?

FABLES launches 20 October 2026. Sink locks from treasury that day and casts the first vote. Deposits and sFABLES open after the audit. vlSINK opens with SINK's launch.
