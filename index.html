What you deposit, what you hold

You deposit FABLES, or a veFABLES NFT you already hold. Sink merges it into one lock at the maximum, with auto-max on, and gives you sFABLES 1:1 on the FABLES inside. The lock behind sFABLES never unlocks. The contract has no switch to turn auto-max off.

Questions

Can I get my FABLES back?

No. The exit is selling sFABLES. The lock has no switch to turn auto-max off.

What keeps sFABLES near one FABLES?

A pool Sink seeds and defends, SINK paid directly to the LPs of that pool, and the treasury buying under par out of its cut.

Is Sink live?

Not yet. The contract is in review. Deposits open inside the claim window, 20 Oct to 20 Nov.

Does holding sFABLES give me a vote?

No. sFABLES earns; vlSINK votes.

Can I deposit a six-month lock?

Yes. It is extended to auto-max on deposit and arrives with the 80% it claimed.
